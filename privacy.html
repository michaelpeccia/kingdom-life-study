Privacy Policy

Kingdom Life Study · Last updated 8 August 2026

Kingdom Life Study does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind in this app.

What the app stores

Everything the app saves stays on your own device, in the app's private storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. This includes:

  • Books you have installed
  • Your highlights
  • Your bookmarks and any notes you write on them
  • Your reading position and display preferences such as theme and text size

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. You can also back it up yourself at any time using Marks → Export, which writes a file you control.

Network use

The app works fully offline. It contacts the network only when you choose to do one of these:

  • Install or update a book. The app requests a study pack file from our content server. No information about you is sent with that request beyond what any web request necessarily includes, such as your device's IP address, which we do not log or retain.
  • Open an external link. Scripture references and the "For Further Study" links open in your browser, on websites we do not operate. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files outside its own storage.

Scripture text

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible, which is in the public domain. Study notes, introductions and commentary are original work, © Kingdom Life.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, that change will be described here before it ships.
